Why Semantic HTML Matters for Accessibility

Published on August 20, 2026 by Senior Dev Mentor

The Power of Built-in Roles

When you use tags like <nav> and <main>, you are providing implicit ARIA roles to the browser. However, adding explicit ARIA attributes ensures compatibility with legacy assistive technologies.

Here are the core tags we are focusing on today:

  • Main: The dominant content of the document.
  • Article: Content that makes sense on its own (like this post).
  • Aside: Content tangentially related to the main content.
